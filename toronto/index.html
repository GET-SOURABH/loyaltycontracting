FAQ

Home Renovation Questions

Useful information for homeowners preparing to discuss a renovation project in Toronto.

What renovation services do you provide in Toronto?

We provide residential renovation services for full-home and multi-room projects, kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and flooring installation.

What types of Toronto homes do you renovate?

Loyalty Contracting has experience working with detached homes, semi-detached homes, townhouses, and condos in Toronto.

Do you renovate Toronto condos?

Yes. We have experience renovating Toronto condos and can help coordinate with condo management regarding approvals, service elevators, work hours, and building requirements. Approval decisions remain with the condo management or board.

Can you help coordinate condo-management requirements?

Yes. We can review the building's renovation rules and help coordinate applicable approval information, service elevator bookings, permitted work hours, and project logistics.

Can you help with renovation permits when required?

When a project requires permits, our team can help coordinate the permitting process, paperwork, and required inspection stages. Not every renovation requires a permit, and inspections are performed by the applicable city authorities.

Can you renovate one room or an entire home?

Yes. Projects can focus on one room or involve several areas of the home. For detailed information about coordinated multi-room work, visit our full home renovation page.
