Brampton Project Experience

Peel Village Renovation Project

Loyalty Contracting completed an open-concept main-floor and kitchen remodel in a detached home in Peel Village, Brampton.

01

Open Main-Floor Layout

On this Peel Village project, a load-bearing wall between the kitchen and dining room was removed to create a more open main-floor layout.

02

Structural Steel Support

The completed project included a concealed structural steel beam supporting the second floor where the wall had been removed.

03

Custom Kitchen

A custom kitchen was built for this home with an 8-foot centre island, shaker-style cabinetry, and quartz countertops.

04

Flooring and Lighting

Main-level flooring was replaced with wide-plank engineered hardwood, and the lighting was updated with LED pot lights as part of this remodel.

What renovation services do you provide in Brampton?
