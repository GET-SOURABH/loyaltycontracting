Mississauga Project Experience

Erin Mills Renovation Project

Loyalty Contracting completed a full basement finishing and legal second suite conversion in a two-storey detached home in Erin Mills, Mississauga.

01

Structural & Layout

For this project, the existing framing was demolished, new load-bearing supports were installed to open the floor plan, and proper egress windows were added.

02

Mechanical Work

The Erin Mills project included a 200-amp electrical-panel upgrade, new HVAC return and supply vents, and a dedicated water heater.

03

Flooring & Soundproofing

Luxury vinyl plank flooring was installed, along with soundproofing insulation in the basement ceiling as part of this project.

04

Kitchenette & Bathroom

The completed suite included custom shaker-style kitchen cabinetry and a full 3-piece bathroom with a walk-in glass shower.

05

Safety & Code Work

Hardwired interconnected smoke and carbon monoxide detectors were installed as part of this project to satisfy applicable secondary-suite requirements.

06

Completed Project Scope

These structural, mechanical, finish, and safety details describe the completed Erin Mills project; requirements vary for other properties and renovation scopes.

Permit Coordination Experience

Permit Coordination on the Erin Mills Project

For this specific Erin Mills project, Loyalty Contracting handled the City of Mississauga building-permit process. This included permit coordination, architectural drawings, HVAC drawings, and submission through the City of Mississauga ePlans portal.

Permit requirements depend on the proposed renovation scope. When permits apply, the team can help coordinate permit-related documentation. The applicable municipality reviews permit submissions and completes any required inspections.
