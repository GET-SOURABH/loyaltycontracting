Each project scope is shaped around the basement that is actually available. Planning these decisions together helps keep the work focused and gives the completed space a consistent direction.

Mississauga Basement Project

Erin Mills Basement Renovation Project

Loyalty Contracting completed a full basement finishing and legal second-suite conversion in a two-storey detached home in Erin Mills, Mississauga.

01

Structural and Layout Work

For this project, the existing framing was demolished, new load-bearing supports were installed to open the floor plan, and egress windows were added.

02

Electrical and Mechanical Work

The Erin Mills project included a 200-amp electrical-panel upgrade, new HVAC return and supply vents, and a dedicated water heater.

03

Flooring and Soundproofing

Luxury Vinyl Plank flooring was installed, together with soundproofing insulation in the basement ceiling as part of this project.

04

Kitchenette and Bathroom

The finished suite included custom shaker-style kitchenette cabinetry and a full three-piece bathroom with a walk-in glass shower.

05

Project Safety Work

Hardwired interconnected smoke detectors and carbon-monoxide detectors were installed as part of this specific second-suite project.

06

A Project-Specific Scope

These structural, electrical, mechanical, finish, and safety details describe the completed Erin Mills project. Other properties and project scopes may require different work.

Permit Coordination Experience

Permit Coordination on the Erin Mills Project

For this specific Erin Mills project, Loyalty Contracting coordinated the City of Mississauga building-permit process. The work included architectural drawings, HVAC drawings, and submission through the City of Mississauga ePlans portal.

Permit requirements depend on the property and proposed renovation scope. When permits apply, the team can help coordinate the related documentation. The applicable municipality reviews permit submissions and completes any required inspections.

Mississauga Basement Renovation Questions

Useful information for homeowners preparing to discuss a basement renovation.

What basement renovation services do you provide in Mississauga?

Basement projects can include finishing, walls and ceilings, flooring, lighting, trims and painting, family or recreation space, home-office and flexible-use areas, storage planning, demolition, preparation, and final detailing based on the agreed scope.

Can you finish an unfinished basement?

Yes. Loyalty Contracting can plan finishing work around the basement's existing condition, intended use, layout priorities, and agreed project scope.

Can a basement be planned for family, office, or recreation space?

A basement can be considered for family and recreation space, a home office, entertainment, guests, storage, a workout area, or flexible use. The suitable options depend on the available space and the specific property.

Do you have experience with a legal second-suite project in Mississauga?

Yes. Loyalty Contracting completed a full basement finishing and legal second-suite conversion in a two-storey detached home in Erin Mills. The work and requirements described on this page apply to that specific project.

Can you help coordinate permit documentation when required?

When permits apply, the team can help coordinate related documentation. For the Erin Mills project, this included architectural drawings, HVAC drawings, and a submission through the City of Mississauga ePlans portal. The municipality reviews submissions and completes any required inspections.

Does every basement qualify for a second-suite conversion?

No universal outcome can be assumed. Feasibility and requirements depend on the property, proposed project scope, and applicable municipal requirements. The Erin Mills example describes one completed project rather than a guarantee for other homes.
