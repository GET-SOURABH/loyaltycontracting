Mississauga Kitchen Project

Port Credit Kitchen Renovation Project

Loyalty Contracting completed a kitchen expansion and modernization in a detached home in Port Credit, Mississauga.

01

Initial Kitchen

Before this project, the home had an outdated 1990s galley kitchen with dark oak cabinets, limited preparation space, poor lighting, and a restrictive wall between the kitchen and dining room.

02

Layout Change

For this Port Credit project, a structural engineer verified that the partition wall was non-load-bearing before it was demolished. Its removal created a more open connection between the kitchen and dining area.

03

Custom Cabinetry

The project included custom ceiling-height shaker cabinetry with soft-close hardware and a dual-tone finish: matte white upper cabinets and navy blue lower cabinets.

04

Countertops and Backsplash

Calacatta quartz countertops were installed with white glazed subway tile arranged in a herringbone pattern for the backsplash.

05

Storage and Island

Storage additions included an integrated pull-out pantry, deep heavy-duty pot drawers, and a concealed garbage and recycling pull-out. The custom 9-foot centre island included an under-mount composite sink, built-in dishwasher, and seating for four.

06

Lighting and Flooring

The lighting plan included LED recessed pot lights, three brass pendant lights above the island, and under-cabinet task lighting. Old cracked ceramic tile was removed and waterproof Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) was installed through the kitchen and dining area.

Project-Specific Electrical Work

Electrical Coordination on the Port Credit Project

For this Port Credit kitchen project, an ESA (Electrical Safety Authority) permit was secured for new dedicated circuits serving the island appliances and the upgraded lighting plan.

FAQ

Mississauga Kitchen Renovation Questions

Useful information for homeowners preparing to discuss a kitchen renovation.

What kitchen renovation services do you provide in Mississauga?

Kitchen projects can include layout and workflow improvements, cabinet installation or replacement, hardware and finish upgrades, storage planning, countertops, backsplash, tile, flooring, lighting, painting, trims, and finishing based on the agreed scope.

Can you renovate a smaller kitchen?

Yes. Planning for a smaller kitchen can focus on practical layouts, efficient cabinetry, useful storage, lighting, work surfaces, and material choices suited to the available space.

Can you improve kitchen layout and storage?

Layout and storage improvements can be considered together after reviewing the existing kitchen, daily workflow, available space, and intended project scope.

Do you install cabinetry, countertops and backsplashes?

Yes. Cabinet installation or replacement, countertops, backsplash, tile, hardware, storage, and finish coordination can form part of an agreed kitchen renovation.

Can an existing kitchen be opened to an adjacent room?

Feasibility depends on the home's existing layout, structure, and proposed scope. For the Port Credit project described on this page, a structural engineer verified that the partition wall was non-load-bearing before demolition.

How do you plan kitchen work around a budget?

Planning starts by identifying priorities and reviewing the proposed layout, materials, and finish choices. Those decisions help shape a practical scope around the homeowner's budget.

Can electrical or permit-related coordination be discussed when required?

Requirements depend on the proposed work. When they apply, permit or electrical coordination can be discussed as part of the renovation scope. On the Port Credit project, an ESA permit was secured for the dedicated island-appliance circuits and upgraded lighting plan.
